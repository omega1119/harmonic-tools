What we build

Harmonic.Tools develops structured music applications for macOS, iOS and iPadOS.

There are three products: Metric for timing and frequency, Modes for music theory and Modes Guitar for the fretboard. Each is available as a Mac app and as an iPhone and iPad app.

Focus areas

Harmonic systems

65 scales and their modes, interval formulas and 20 guitar tunings.

Compositional tools

Diatonic chords with Roman numerals and function labels, borrowed chords, and the circles of fifths and thirds for planning modulations.

Analysis & reference

Lookups for intervals, keys, chords and scales, plus note frequencies, delay times and the harmonic series.

Instrument & input

MIDI keyboard input, a fretboard view of scales and chords, and a strobe tuner.

Products

Timing and frequency tools, a music theory app and a guitar fretboard app, each for Mac and for iPhone and iPad.

Philosophy

Music can be approached as a structured system.

Harmonic.Tools builds apps that show how notes, scales, chords and tempos relate to each other.

About
