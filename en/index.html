Philosophy

Music can be approached as a structured system.

Harmonic.Tools builds software that exposes musical relationships clearly and directly.

About

Harmonic.Tools is a long-term music software lab focused on structured, system-level tools for Apple platforms.

We build with Swift and SwiftUI using modern macOS and iOS technologies. The emphasis is on precision, stability and clarity.
