Metric for macOS

A windowed timing and frequency toolkit for macOS with a built-in metronome. It sets delay times from a tempo, calculates FM ratios and sidebands, syncs an LFO to the beat and transposes a sample, in a resizable window you can keep open beside your DAW. Siri, Shortcuts and a Notification Center widget are supported.

  • Metronome with tap tempo & visual beats
  • BPM → ms / Hz converter (straight, dotted, triplet)
  • Note ↔ Frequency table (108 notes, A4 = 440 Hz)
  • Transpose & Speed, FM Calculator, LFO Rates, 32 Harmonics
  • Menu bar tap tempo, delay times in ms or Hz, plus a metronome and notes
  • Siri, Shortcuts & a Notification Center BPM widget

Features

  • The metronome has a tempo dial, tap-to-set tempo, time signatures, subdivisions and a visual beat indicator.
  • Click the large tap target to measure tempo. Metric shows the BPM from your last tap and a rolling average over up to eight taps, and either reading can go straight into the BPM Converter, Transpose or LFO Rate tool with one click.
  • The BPM → ms / Hz converter takes a tempo and gives straight, dotted and triplet values for whole, half, quarter, eighth and sixteenth notes. Every row shows milliseconds and frequency in Hz, so you can set delay, reverb pre-delay or sidechain timing without a calculator.
  • The note frequency table lists all 108 chromatic notes from C0 to B8. Each note shows its MIDI number, frequency in Hz, period in milliseconds, and sample counts at 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz and 96 kHz. Middle C is flagged. Based on A4 = 440 Hz.
  • For transposing, pick an original key and a target key to see the semitone shift, interval name and exact DAW transpose value. Add an original BPM and Metric works out the new tempo and playback speed ratio. A cents slider (±100) and a speed slider covering two octaves (±24 semitones) allow fine adjustment.
  • The FM calculator has two modes. Ratio Explorer sets carrier and modulator ratios from a base frequency and offers ten FM presets, from hollow octaves (1:1) to inharmonic bells (1:π). Pitch Correction works in reverse: pick a target note and get the exact carrier and modulator frequencies. Both modes show a sideband table of up to ten partials with their frequencies, nearest notes and colour-coded amplitudes, driven by a modulation index slider.
  • The LFO rate calculator converts any BPM into LFO rates across 17 musical divisions, from four bars down to 1/32 triplets. Each row shows Hz, period in milliseconds, and samples per cycle at 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz and 96 kHz. Reverse lookup takes any Hz value and finds the closest musical division, with the error shown.
  • The harmonic series explorer starts from a frequency or any note and lists the first 32 harmonics with frequency, nearest note name, cents deviation and interval label. Low-order harmonics are colour-coded, and deviations greater than 30 cents are highlighted, which makes inharmonic content easy to spot.

Shared tempo and tuning

Tap a tempo once and the BPM Converter, Transpose and LFO Rate tools all use the same live BPM. Note Frequency, FM Calculator and Harmonics all use A4 = 440 Hz.

Siri, Shortcuts & widget

Ask Siri to start or stop the metronome, set a BPM, tap a tempo, convert BPM to ms, find a note frequency or transpose a tempo. Every tool is a Shortcuts action too, and the BPM widget keeps your tempo in Notification Center.

Menu bar tools

Tap a tempo from the menu bar without opening the app. Right-click for delay times in ms or Hz, and type in any tempo. Metronome and note pitch items are optional, and there are menu-bar-only and open-at-login settings.

On the desktop

  • Runs entirely offline. No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Native macOS app with sidebar navigation
  • Dark-mode aware interface that follows your system appearance
  • Sample counts at 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz and 96 kHz
  • Resizable window. Keep it open beside your DAW

Accessibility

Works with VoiceOver and Voice Control, and can be used from the keyboard, with arrow keys and Space.
